So what do I do?

I'm Luis a UX researcher and UI designer who believes design should make sense before it makes noise. My work sits at the intersection of how people think, feel, and move through digital systems — and how those systems can evolve without losing their humanity.


Some of my work

User Experience (UX) research is about investigating human behavior, what people actually do, not what they say they do. It involves observing patterns, identifying pain points, and uncovering opportunities for improvement. Through interviews, usability tests, analytics, and behavioral insights.

01. Concept: Trek Watch 91 UI

The TW91 interface is designed for quick, at-a-glance interactions during outdoor activities. It prioritizes legibility, simplicity, and ease of use, ensuring that users can access essential information without distraction.

02. Moto Digital Dash

Designing a glanceable, safety-critical interface for motorcycles Role: UX Research · UI Design · Interaction Design Context: High vibration, sunlight, gloves, motion, zero tolerance for distraction

03. MMigrating page to new design

Revamping and migrating an existing webpage to a new design system, ensuring consistency in user experience and visual aesthetics across the platform.
